Typical workloads

  • Promo window batch (fal, 100 clips × 6 s @ $0.0425/s): 600 s × $0.0425 = $25.50; at list it would be $102 — lock in volume before Nov 7.
  • Social content farm (fal list, 50 clips/day × 8 s): 400 s × $0.17 = $68/day → $2,040/mo.
  • BFL FHD short film (40 clips × 10 s @ $0.24/s): 400 s → $96 one-off, audio included.
  • Iterate in drafts (BFL, 5 draft variants × 10 s @ $0.06/s = $3, then one UHD enhance 10 s × $0.41 = $4.10) — cheaper than 6 full UHD renders ($24.60).

Cost-cutting levers

  • Front-load work before Nov 7 — the fal promo is a hard date gate; anything exploratory belongs in the promo window.
  • Draft-then-enhance on BFL: drafts cost ~40% of a full HD render and render faster; you only pay full price for the shot you keep.
  • Stay at HD for iteration, upscale once — FHD/QHD/UHD are the same generation finished by the upsampler and billed at the delivered class, so commit to 4K only on final cuts.
  • t2v/i2v vs v2v: video-conditioned generations cost ~40% more per second on BFL — prefer image-to-video when a still can pin the opening frame.

FAQ

Is FLUX 3 Max available on the BFL API?

No. FLUX 3 Max is fal's exclusive post-trained version (fal Research continued training FLUX 3 for fidelity, motion coherence and prompt adherence). It runs on fal's infrastructure. BFL's own API sells the standard FLUX 3 Video models at the HD–UHD rates above.

Does FLUX 3 charge extra for audio?

No. Synchronized audio (dialogue, ambience, effects) is included in the per-second rate on both channels. BFL's marketing headline is "audio included at no extra charge."

Why is the fal promo price so much lower than BFL's?

fal is running a 75%-off launch promotion to drive adoption of its post-trained model — $0.0425/s vs its own $0.17/s list. It ends November 7, 2026; plan post-promo budgets at list price (the calculator includes it).

How long can clips be?

5–20 seconds per generation on FLUX 3 (video continuation up to 15 s), at 24 fps, aspect ratios from 21:9 to 9:21, delivered up to 3840 × 2176 for 16:9 UHD.
