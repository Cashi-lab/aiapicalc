FAQ

Is Nano Banana 2.1 the same as Gemini 3.1 Flash Image?

NB 2.1 is the update to Nano Banana 2 (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image) — improved quality, character consistency, text rendering, and search-grounded generation at 1K/2K/4K.

Why does image output cost "per 1M tokens"?

Images are tokenized: a 1K image ≈ 1,120 output tokens, 2K ≈ 1,680, 4K ≈ 3,767 — that's where $0.0336/$0.0504/$0.113 come from.

Free tier?

Not available for NB 2.1 output; it's paid-tier only.
