Cost levers

  • Caching is the big one — $4 → $0.20 per cached 1M; at 80% hit rate a 500k-token workload drops from $2.16 to ~$1.12/request.
  • Cap reasoning output — output (incl. thinking) at $20/M is where bills explode; bound max tokens on agentic loops.
  • Use Muse Spark for cheap tiers — Meta's Spark family ($1.25 in / $4.25 out) handles routine traffic at ~1/4 the cost.

FAQ

Why is Muse Wonder so expensive?

It's Meta's frontier reasoning tier (comparable slots run $4–$20). The reasoning quality is the product; cache + output caps are how you keep it affordable.

Muse Wonder vs Muse Spark pricing?

Wonder: $4/$20 per 1M. Spark: $1.25/$4.25. Route accordingly.

Is there a free tier?

Check dev.meta.ai for current trial credits; OpenRouter lists no free Wonder endpoint.
