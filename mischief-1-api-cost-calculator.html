FAQ

How much does the Mischief 1 API cost?

$0.005 per second at 480p and $0.01 per second at 720p — a 5-second clip is $0.025 or $0.05. Identical on OpenRouter and fal.

Is Mischief 1 the cheapest video API?

On OpenRouter, yes (as of Nov 1, 2026). The next cheapest at 480p — HeyGen Video 1 and Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Lite — cost $0.02/s, four times more.

Does Mischief 1 really run on brain cells?

No. TBC grows real neuron networks in its own lab as a research tool; what ships is ordinary software on standard AI hardware — a post-training layer around a diffusion transformer adding under 0.1% of parameters.

What resolutions and durations does it support?

Image-to-video only: one image as the first frame, 3s or 5s clips, 480p or 720p, 16:9, no sound.
