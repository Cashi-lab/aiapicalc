Cost levers

  • Shift to off-peak — every rate is exactly half: batch jobs, evals, and crawls scheduled on weekend/late-night Beijing time cost 50% less.
  • KV-cache aggressively — cache-hit input is ¥0.02 vs ¥1 miss (50×). Agentic loops that resend the same system prompt + tools hit hardest.
  • 1M context ≠ free resends — long documents re-bill as input every call; keep them at the cached prefix, not the tail.

FAQ

What model name do I call for V4.1 Flash?

deepseek-flash. Legacy names deepseek-v4-flash and deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp still resolve but route to V4.1-Flash-1017 at Flash prices. V4-Pro is being phased out — its requests route to V4.1-Flash at Flash rates until V4.1-Pro launches.

Does DeepSeek bill in USD?

Official list prices are in CNY (¥). USD figures here are converted at the editable FX rate above (default 7.10). Third-party gateways quoting "$0.15 in / $0.60 out" are reselling the off-peak CNY rates.

Is V4.1 Flash multimodal?

Yes — native image understanding is supported on the API; image tokens bill as input. FIM completion and prefix writing are in beta on the same model.

How fast is it?

A 552B-parameter asymmetric MoE with 8B active input / 16B output parameters, tuned for high throughput; KV cache needs 1/4 the HBM of the previous generation.
