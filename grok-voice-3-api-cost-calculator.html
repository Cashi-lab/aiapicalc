How to cut your bill

  • Keep prompts short — every chat turn re-sends context at $4/M tokens; a 20k-token system prompt on 100 calls/day costs $8/day alone.
  • Bound conversation length — audio is billed per second; cap sessions to avoid runaway sessions.
  • Avoid PSTN when possible — WebRTC sessions skip the $0.10/min carrier fee, you only pay the $0.08/min audio price.

FAQ

Is there a free tier for grok-voice-3.0?

No. xAI realtime voice models are pay-as-you-go only (team credit required). Text-side free tiers do not apply to the realtime audio endpoint.

How is audio billed — tokens or seconds?

Both views exist in xAI billing: realtimeAudioSecondPrice ($0.0133/s) and realtimeAudioTokenPrice ($0.267/M audio tokens). They are the same cost expressed in different units; this calculator uses the per-second/minute view.

Does Grok Voice 3.0 cost more than 2.0?

No — same published rates. See the comparison section above.

What about text output tokens?

The realtime endpoint returns audio; text output is not separately metered on the voice channel. Text-in (your prompts, tool results) is metered at $4/M.
