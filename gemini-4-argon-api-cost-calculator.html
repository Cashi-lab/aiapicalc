The 1M-token context reality check

Gemini 4 Argon accepts up to 1M tokens. A single full-context request (1M in, 4k out) costs $2.04 at launch pricing — and $4.08 after Dec 1. That is cheap per request, but agentic loops re-send context hundreds of times a day, which is exactly why implicit caching matters.

Three levers that actually cut cost

  • Implicit caching — repeated prefixes (system prompt, RAG docs, repo context) drop from $2/M to $0.10/M. At an 80% hit rate, that same 1M-token request falls from $2.04 to ~$0.52 — a 4× cut that compounds across every agent step.
  • Batch API — 50% off for non-latency-critical workloads (evals, enrichment, backfills).
  • Trim thinking tokens — output is billed including reasoning tokens; cap max_output / thinking budget on agentic loops where 1.5k visible tokens may hide 10k thinking tokens.

FAQ

Is Gemini 4 Argon available on the free tier?

No. It is paid-only, and also excluded from the Google AI Pro plan. You need a billed API project.

Does Argon support explicit caching?

No — implicit caching only, billed at $0.10/M (launch) for cached portions of the input.

Is there a long-context surcharge (>200k tokens)?

Unlike some Gemini models with dual-tier rates, Argon's published table shows a single standard rate for input/output regardless of context length, up to 1M tokens.

Thinking tokens really are billed?

Yes — the output price explicitly includes thinking tokens. Budget agents on total generated tokens, not just visible text.
